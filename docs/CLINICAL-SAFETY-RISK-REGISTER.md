# MediLink — Clinical-Safety & Security Risk Register

Risks derived from the high-risk workflows identified in BuildPlan §2 (and
related sections). Likelihood/impact are pre-migration assessments as of
August 2026; each risk is re-assessed when its mitigation lands and before
pilot release. Owner roles are unassigned until Phase 0 assigns named
owners (BuildPlan §12 Phase 0).

| ID | Risk | Category | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner role | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CS-01 | Wrong-patient identity merge: two profiles merged in error, or records written to the wrong patient, producing a chart that mixes two people's clinical data | clinical-safety | Medium | Critical | Reversible, audited merge/split case workflow with human review (ADR-0002); identity-caution state blocks high-risk writes; patient context bar with "wrong patient?" escape; Add record only inside authorized patient workspace with encounter context; demographic confirmation before writes | Clinical-safety lead — unassigned | Open — mitigation not yet built (BuildPlan §14 B) |
| CS-02 | Patient directory enumeration via name/phone search or ID probing exposes existence and demographics of patients | security/privacy | High | High | Remove name/phone doctor search after patient-present handoff exists (ADR-0008); rate-limited ID/ABHA lookup; "a record may exist" response before authorization with no name/DOB/city/phone/record-count leakage; enumeration and pre-consent leakage tests | Security owner — unassigned | Open — current `patientService.search()` still searches name/phone |
| CS-03 | Break-glass / emergency access abuse: QR or ID used to reveal health data without justification; repeated unjustified emergency events | security/privacy | Medium | Critical | Separate high-assurance emergency policy: step-up MFA, structured + free-text reason with minimum length, opaque signed single-use QR tokens, per-doctor/patient/org/IP-device rate limits, minimal data set, per-read auditing, instant and out-of-band patient notification, trust-and-safety review SLA, abuse flags blocking new events | Clinical-safety lead — unassigned + Security owner — unassigned | Open — current implementation lacks step-up auth, throttling, review SLAs |
| CS-04 | Absence-of-data misread: "No allergies documented" interpreted as "no allergies" in an emergency or AI summary | clinical-safety | High | Critical | Exact wording policy: "No allergy record found in MediLink as of [date]"; bold coverage caveat in emergency profile; AI prohibited from declaring a patient safe/normal/clear from absent data; record coverage and last-updated indicators always shown | Clinical-safety lead — unassigned | Open — copy policy defined, AI red-team suite not yet built (Phase 8) |
| CS-05 | Consent misunderstanding: patient approves a grant without understanding requester, purpose, scope, exclusions, or expiry; or treats request approval as controlling provider writes | privacy | High | High | Request card answering who/why/what/not-what/how-long/what-if-decline in order; no preselected scopes — patient selects every category; requester suggestions labelled as such; authoring authority separated from disclosure consent (ADR-0004); ≥90% comprehension target in moderated usability tests | Product owner — unassigned | Open — `access_requests` state machine not yet built |
| CS-06 | Overwritten corrections: an "amended" record silently replaces the original, destroying evidence and hiding the correction history | clinical-safety | Medium | High | Immutable record versions with lifecycle status (corrected/superseded/entered-in-error/disputed), correction reason, and `replacesVersionId`; originals remain readable under a "Superseded" banner; patient dispute flow flags disputed high-risk facts in the critical band; DB-level immutability on version rows | Engineering lead — unassigned | Open — current `superseded_by` model is insufficient (BuildPlan §9.3) |
| CS-07 | Lab reference range misuse: generic thresholds or incompatible units/methods used to declare a value "normal" or joined into a misleading trend | clinical-safety | Medium | High | Preserve source lab's own range and flag; render range bar only for numeric value + compatible numeric source range + compatible unit; no recalculated normal flags; no cross-unit/method trend joining; plain-language explanation of source flags; shape and text carry meaning beyond color | Clinical-safety lead — unassigned | Open — unit/range compatibility logic not yet built |
| CS-08 | AI hallucination: summary states facts absent from sources, turns recorded data into conclusions/diagnoses/recommendations, or leaks another patient's context | clinical-safety | High | High | Claim contract with version IDs/pages/hashes and consent scope snapshot; missing citation prevents display; prohibited-task list (no diagnosis, triage, dosage, safety-from-absence); renamed SBAR sections (Recorded critical facts, etc.); red-team suite for hallucination, prompt injection, cross-patient retrieval, scope leakage; stale summaries labelled | Clinical-safety lead — unassigned + Engineering lead — unassigned | Open — Phase 8; deterministic summaries only today |
| CS-09 | Lab batch confirmation error: batch operation attests multiple clinical reports or wrong-patient fields at once | clinical-safety | Medium | High | Batch pre-fill only for homogeneous non-clinical metadata after sampled review; individual attestation required per patient report; candidate fields with page evidence and Confirm/Correct/Exclude controls; "unable to verify" path delivering source as `structured_data_unavailable` | Clinical-safety lead — unassigned | Open — lab review queue not yet built |
| CS-10 | Glass/transparency accessibility failure: translucent panels or dark-only themes make clinical data unreadable in bright clinics, on poor displays, on printouts, or for low-vision/forced-colors users; legible data behind overlays leaks to bystanders | clinical-safety (accessibility) | Medium | Medium | Daylight opaque operational theme + Print theme shipped from foundation (ADR-0007); glass restricted to shell and ≤2 transient layers, never over dense data; opaque `backdrop-filter` fallbacks; reduce-transparency preference; WCAG 2.2 AA verified in all modes incl. forced-colors; textual status alongside all color | Design lead — unassigned | Open — token layers and theme harness pending (BuildPlan §14 E) |

## Register rules

- No risk may be closed without evidence (test results, usability study, or
  tabletop record) attached; "it renders" is not done (BuildPlan §13).
- Likelihood/impact are re-scored at each phase gate and before the
  real-patient pilot decision (BuildPlan §17).
- Security-owned risks additionally track penetration-test findings; each
  finding is resolved or formally risk-accepted by the accountable owner
  before launch.
